### 🏗️ Professional Profile

I am a Civil & Environmental Engineer with a growing focus on **Data Science, Artificial Intelligence and intelligent infrastructure systems**.

My engineering background has exposed me to different aspects of the built environment, including **transportation, highway construction, engineering surveying, structural design and water-related systems**. These experiences developed my interest not only in how infrastructure is designed and constructed, but also in how data and technology can be used to understand, manage and improve it.

Today, I am developing the ability to approach infrastructure problems from both the **engineering and computational perspectives**. I use data to identify patterns, investigate real-world problems and explore how machine learning and intelligent systems can support better engineering decisions.

My core area of interest is **Transportation Engineering and Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS)**, particularly the application of data and AI to traffic management, transportation safety and mobility. At the same time, I am interested in broader applications across **infrastructure, structural systems, water and hydraulic engineering, construction and the built environment**.

Ultimately, I want to work on problems where **engineering knowledge, data and computational intelligence come together to create safer, more efficient and more sustainable infrastructure**.
